Word-perfect highlighting

Thousands of voices

Search a catalogue of millions by name or by feel — deep, calm, British, dramatic. Preview instantly, favourite the ones you love, pick a different narrator per book.

It reads books, not files

Copyright pages, tables of contents and indexes are skipped. PDF page numbers, running headers and hyphen-split words are repaired. Chapters are detected automatically.

Questions

Is it really free?

The app is free and open source. Narration runs on your own Fish Audio account, which has a free tier — so casual reading typically costs nothing.

Why do I need my own narration key?

Because there's no middleman server. Your text goes straight from your Mac to the voice API with your key, so nobody else sees what you read, and your usage is yours to control.

Does it work offline?

Anything already narrated is cached and plays offline forever. New text needs a connection the first time it's spoken.

Which devices work?

Any modern browser — iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac or Windows. There is also an optional Mac app. On the Mac app's first launch, right-click it and choose Open: it's signed, but not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS asks once.

Does my book get uploaded anywhere?

Your library, reading position and generated audio stay on your device. The one thing that leaves is the passage being narrated: it's sent to Fish Audio to be turned into speech, because that's where the voices run. Full detail in the privacy policy.

How do updates work?

Fish Reader checks for new versions on launch and updates itself with one click. You never reinstall it manually.
