Reading that keeps up with you

Word-perfect highlighting

Each sentence lights up, and the exact word being spoken is marked as it's said — so your eyes never lose the line. Built for dyslexic and ADHD readers first.

Thousands of voices

Search a catalogue of millions by name or by feel — deep, calm, British, dramatic. Preview instantly, favourite the ones you love, pick a different narrator per book.

It reads books, not files

Copyright pages, tables of contents and indexes are skipped. PDF page numbers, running headers and hyphen-split words are repaired. Chapters are detected automatically.

Never pays twice

Every sentence is cached on your Mac the first time it's spoken. Re-reading, scrubbing back and jumping chapters is instant — and free.

Keeps playing

Leave the book and a mini player follows you. Pop out a floating window that stays on top. Media keys, AirPods and a sleep timer all work like you'd expect.

Private by design

Your books, progress and audio live on your Mac — never on our servers, because there are no servers. You connect your own narration account.

Running in two minutes

  1. Download and drag Fish Reader to Applications.
  2. Connect a free Fish Audio key — the app walks you through it.
  3. Import a book and press play. That's the whole setup.

Questions

Is it really free?

The app is free and open source. Narration runs on your own Fish Audio account, which has a free tier — so casual reading typically costs nothing.

Why do I need my own narration key?

Because there's no middleman server. Your text goes straight from your Mac to the voice API with your key, so nobody else sees what you read, and your usage is yours to control.

Does it work offline?

Anything already narrated is cached and plays offline forever. New text needs a connection the first time it's spoken.

Which Macs are supported?

Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer) on macOS 11 or later. On first launch, right-click the app and choose Open — the build isn't notarized by Apple yet.

How do updates work?

Fish Reader checks for new versions on launch and updates itself with one click. You never reinstall it manually.
