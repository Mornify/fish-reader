Privacy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.
  • Your books, reading progress and generated audio are stored on your own device, never on our servers.
  • Text you press play on is sent to Fish Audio to be turned into speech. That is how narration works and it cannot be avoided.
  • You use your own Fish Audio API key. It is stored on your device and passed straight through to Fish Audio.
  • Our narration relay does not store your key, your text or your audio.
  • You can delete everything at any time from Settings, or by clearing site data in your browser.

Who runs this

What is stored on your device

All of the following stays in your browser (or in the app's folder on macOS) and is never uploaded:

WhatWhereWhy
Your Fish Audio API keylocalStorageTo narrate without asking you to paste it every time
Imported books and documentsIndexedDBSo your library is there when you come back
Generated narration audioIndexedDBSo re-reading is instant and doesn't cost you again
Reading position, bookmarks, saved voices, settingslocalStorageTo continue where you left off

Under the EU ePrivacy rules, storage that is strictly necessary to provide a service you explicitly asked for does not require a consent banner. Everything listed above exists solely to make the reader work — there is no analytics, profiling or advertising storage of any kind. We still tell you about it on first visit, because you should know where your data lives.

What leaves your device

Fish Audio

When you press play, the text of the passage being read and your API key are sent to Fish Audio so it can generate speech. Fish Audio is an independent company and processes that request under its own privacy policy and terms. If you would rather no text left your device at all, do not use narration.

Our narration relay

Web browsers are not allowed to call the Fish Audio speech endpoint directly, so the web version passes that one request through a small relay we host. The relay forwards your request and streams the audio back. It does not store your key, your text or your audio, keeps no database, and does not log request contents.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the relay runs on Cloudflare. Like any website, those providers process your IP address and request metadata in order to serve you the page — this is ordinary infrastructure logging, not something we collect or have access to.

What we never do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or passwords.
  • No analytics or measurement scripts. No Google Fonts, no CDNs, no embedded third-party scripts of any kind.
  • No advertising, no profiling, no selling or sharing of data — we hold none to sell.
  • No copies of your books on any server we run.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/UK you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under the GDPR. In practice we hold essentially no personal data about you, so there is usually nothing for us to return or delete — the data is on your device and entirely under your control:

  • See it: it is your library, visible in the app.
  • Export it: your books came from your own files; they are unchanged on your device.
  • Delete it: Settings → Disconnect removes your key; Settings → Clear cache removes generated audio; removing a book deletes it. Clearing site data in your browser erases everything at once.

For text that has already been sent to Fish Audio, those rights are exercised with Fish Audio directly, as they are the ones processing it.

If you are in California, the CCPA/CPRA rights to know, delete and opt out of sale apply. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Fish Reader is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. It also requires a Fish Audio account, which has its own age terms.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and the first-visit notice is shown again. Past versions are visible in the project's public git history.
